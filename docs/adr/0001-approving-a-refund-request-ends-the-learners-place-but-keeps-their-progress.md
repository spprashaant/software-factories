# Approving a Refund Request ends the learner's place but keeps their progress

When Support Approves a Refund Request, the learner loses their place on the course (unless another Individual Purchase that isn't Refunded, or a claimed team seat, still gives them one), but their lesson progress, watch history and quiz attempts are kept. Access follows the money only once Support has decided, and keeping progress means a learner who buys again picks up where they left off, while deleting it could never be undone.

## Considered Options

- **End access when the Refund Request is raised**: rejected, because a Declined Refund Request would have cut off a learner who is still paying, and Support would have to restore their place by hand.
- **Delete progress on Approval**: rejected, because it destroys data that has nothing to do with the money and cannot be recovered if the learner buys again.

# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary fixes the words we use for buying a course and for giving the money back.

## Buying and refunding

**Purchase**:
A learner's payment for one course, at the price they were charged.
_Avoid_: Order, transaction

**Refunded Purchase**:
A Purchase whose money has been given back by a Refund; it no longer gives the learner access to the course.
_Avoid_: Cancelled purchase, reversed purchase

**Refund**:
Money Cadence gives back to a learner for a whole Purchase; there are no partial Refunds. A Refund exists only once a Refund Request is Approved. It is the only word for this, in the product, in emails and in code.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, cancel my purchase, reversal, chargeback, pending refund, partial refund

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask for a Refund on one Purchase, with their reason in their own words. Learners make one by choosing "Request a refund". It is Requested until Support Approves or Declines it; a Declined Refund Request is not a Refund. Each Refund Request is separate: after a Decline, the learner may make a new one while they still have Refund Eligibility, but a Purchase has at most one Requested at a time.
_Avoid_: Pending chargeback, reversal request, cancellation, refund (for the ask itself), appeal

**Approve**:
Support's decision to grant a Refund Request, which creates a Refund.
_Avoid_: Accept, grant, process

**Decline**:
Support's decision not to grant a Refund Request; no money is given back.
_Avoid_: Reject, deny, refuse

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a learner may make a Refund Request for a Purchase right now. It requires being inside the Refund Window, not having watched or completed too much of the course, and the Purchase not being a claimed team seat.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer, refundable

**Refund Window**:
The period from the Purchase day to the end of the 14th calendar day after it, during which the learner may make a Refund Request. A Purchase on 1 November can be refunded through 15 November. It is one condition of Refund Eligibility.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cooling-off period

**Refund Amount**:
The money a Refund gives back: always the amount the learner paid for the Purchase.
_Avoid_: Refundable amount, reversal amount, list price

**Chargeback**:
A dispute a learner raises with their bank or card issuer to claw back a payment, bypassing Cadence. It is not a Refund and not a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Using it for anything a learner asks Cadence for directly

## People

**Support**:
The job of deciding Refund Requests (Approving or Declining them), done by Platform Admins. Support is a job, not a separate kind of account.
_Avoid_: Reviewer, ops, team Admin

**Platform Admin**:
A Cadence staff account with authority over the whole platform. Platform Admins do the job of Support.
_Avoid_: Admin on its own (ambiguous with the team Admin), superuser

**Team Admin**:
The person who manages a team and its team-licence seats. A Team Admin has no say over Refund Requests.
_Avoid_: Admin on its own, Support
